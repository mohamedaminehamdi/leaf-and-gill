    About Leaf & Gill

    Works with no signal. BioCLIP 2, an open model trained on 200 million biology photos, runs on your phone. The first visit downloads it once (609 MB); after that it works in airplane mode. Photos never leave the phone.

    Warns about deadly look-alikes. A hand-written list of toxic genera (death cap, poison hemlock, false morel…) is checked against every answer and its known look-alikes.

    Knows when it's guessing. A trust meter learned with TabPFN on 3,000 fresh iNaturalist photos says "not sure" instead of bluffing.

    Never eat anything based on an app.
