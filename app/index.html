Starting…
Works with no signal

BioCLIP 2, an open model trained on 200 million biology photos, runs on your phone. Your photos never leave it.
Warns about deadly look-alikes

Death cap, poison hemlock, false morel… even when the photo looks like something harmless.
Knows when it's guessing

A trust meter, learned with TabPFN on 3,000 fresh photos, says "not sure" instead of bluffing.
11,896 plants & fungi from GBIF · model BioCLIP 2 (MIT) · trust meter built with TabPFN · source code
